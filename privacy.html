This policy explains what (“we”, “us”) collects when you use this website and app, and how it is used. By using the site you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

  • Email address – only if you join the Pro waitlist or contact us.
  • Technical data – such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, recorded automatically in the server logs of our hosting provider.
  • Browser storage – small items saved on your own device, such as your cookie choice.
  • Analytics and advertising data – if we turn on tools like Google Analytics or Google AdSense, they may use cookies to measure visits and show ads. Where the law requires your consent, we use non-essential cookies only after you accept.

2. Instagram account data

The follower counts shown on this site are public numbers about public Instagram accounts, obtained from third-party data providers. We do not collect information about the followers of those accounts, and we never ask for your Instagram login or password.

3. How we use information

To run and improve the site, keep it secure, tell waitlist members about the Pro plan, show ads or sponsor placements, and meet legal obligations.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site: hosting, data providers, analytics and advertising partners (such as Google), and payment processors when Pro launches. We may also disclose information if required by law. We do not sell your email address.

5. Cookies and consent

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies in the banner shown on your first visit. You can change your choice by clearing this site’s data in your browser. Google’s ad personalisation can be managed at adssettings.google.com.

6. How long we keep data

Waitlist emails are kept until you ask us to delete them or the waitlist is closed. Server logs are kept for the period set by our hosting provider.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, withdraw consent, or unsubscribe at any time by emailing . We will reply within a reasonable time.

8. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Security and transfers

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is perfectly secure. Our providers may store data in other countries.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
